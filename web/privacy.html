Your choices and legal basis

Optional demo-message processing uses your affirmative permission; declining leaves portfolio browsing and deterministic examples available. It is separate from your cookie choice and lasts only on the current page. Withdraw using the reset control to prevent further AI requests and clear the page’s conversation; withdrawal does not undo previous processing. Do not send health records, identifiers, confidential material or other sensitive personal information.

Where GDPR applies, necessary website delivery and proportionate abuse prevention rely on legitimate interests in providing a secure service. Responding to a requested professional engagement may involve steps at your request before a contract, or legitimate interests in responding to correspondence. Optional AI processing relies on consent. No marketing mailing list or automated client-selection decision is operated.

Providers and international processing

Vercel hosts the website and API; Amazon Web Services supplies Bedrock inference using Amazon Nova Lite. The configured US inference profile processes requests in the United States, and hosting infrastructure may operate internationally. LinkedIn and your email provider process information you choose to send to them. These providers are not advertising recipients installed on this website.

Provider contracts, retention and applicable international-transfer safeguards must be assessed for any personal-data engagement. This public portfolio is intended for fictional or public demo inputs; its consent prompt is not a substitute for GDPR Chapter V transfer safeguards. Do not use it as a production service for personal records.

Provider information: Vercel privacy notice, Bedrock data protection, Bedrock abuse detection, and LinkedIn privacy policy.

Adults, security and updates

These professional AI demos are intended for people aged 18 or over. No child-targeted profiling or collection is intended. HTTPS, server-side cloud credentials, bounded message sizes and best-effort rate limits protect the demos. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Material changes to processing will require updating this notice and the permission step as appropriate.

India’s DPDP Act and Rules have phased commencement. Applicable obligations depend on the activity, jurisdiction and provisions in force. This notice and the controls describe this deployment; they are not a certification of complete DPDP or GDPR compliance.
